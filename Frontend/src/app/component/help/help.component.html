Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

You can track your order in real time by going to the “My Orders” section. Each order shows live updates on delivery status.

I received the wrong order. What should I do?

We're sorry about that! Please go to “Help” in the app and report the issue. Our support team will resolve it quickly.

What are the available payment methods?

You can pay using Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Wallets like Paytm or PhonePe, and Cash on Delivery in most areas.

How do I cancel an order?

Go to “My Orders” → select the order → click on “Cancel”. Note: Cancellation may not be possible once the order is prepared.
