About Swiggy

Swiggy is India's leading on-demand food delivery platform connecting millions of customers with their favorite restaurants, cafes, and cloud kitchens across the country.

Our Journey

Founded in 2014, Swiggy started with a simple mission — to bring delicious food right to your doorstep. What began as a small startup in Bengaluru has now expanded to over 500 cities, redefining convenience and creating countless opportunities for delivery partners and restaurants.
